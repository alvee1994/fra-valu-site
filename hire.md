I'm now doing an MBA at Rotterdam School of Management alongside it. I'm looking for a full-time role doing the same kind of work: finding what eats a team's time, and building the fix.</p>

    <p>Fravalu shows how I work best. Every project runs the same way. First, map how the team spends its week. Then price what each repeated task costs.     <p>Before Fravalu, I joined a five-person MBA team advising Truvalu, a global impact investor. The goal: grow its Colombia portfolio from 21 to 45-50 companies. I led the search for the right AI tools and tested them by hand. I designed a five-stage monitoring workflow, then built the automations behind it: agents that pull data from messy documents, agents wired into Google Drive, and an automated intake form. Faculty and the client both rated the work highly.</p>

    <p>Before that, I was a Solutions Architect at Mesh Bio. I led a team building healthcare data systems across five Southeast Asian markets. I built automated data pipelines on AWS that raised productivity 80%. I owned delivery end to end. I sat between engineering, sales, and regional partners, and turned vague business needs into specs engineers could build from.</p>
