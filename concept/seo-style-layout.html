One accountable program

Fravalu is an AI and workflow automation consultancy for small and mid-sized businesses that do not have a data team of their own. We combine process mapping, automation design, and measurement into one program: a ranked, costed plan for what is worth automating, and a build that proves the first item on it.

01

The short answer

What does an automation partner actually do?

An automation partner finds where a business loses hours to repeated manual work, and separates the processes worth automating from the ones that already run well enough. It turns that judgement into a plan you can act on, then builds the highest-value piece of it.

We start with your team, not a tool. We sit with the people doing the work, map every repeated task, and price what each one costs in hours and money. Only then do we recommend what to automate, in what order, and what it should cost to build.

We build with the plain, widely used tools your team can maintain: scripts, cloud functions, and the AI models suited to each task, wired into the systems you already run. Nothing proprietary, and nothing that locks you to us.

Automation is the output, not the pitch. Some of what we find, we recommend you leave alone, because the hours saved would not cover the build.

Key takeaways
  • We diagnose before we quote. The plan is yours whether you build with us or not.
  • Every recommendation carries a price and an estimated saving, ranked against the rest.
  • We build inside your existing tools. No proprietary platform, no lock-in.
  • Leaving a process alone is a valid recommendation, and we say so when it applies.
02

How we work

Diagnose, then build the one thing that pays back first.

Every engagement runs the same way, in this order. Skipping a stage is how automation projects end up expensive and unused.

We map your week, put a price on the ranked list, and build the highest-value workflow first, so you see the return before committing to more.

Map. We sit with your team and log every repeated task: the reports, the copy and paste between systems, the chasing, the manual entry.

Rank and price. Each process gets an estimated saving and a fixed build price. You see the full list before agreeing to anything.

Build. We build the workflow that pays back first, inside the tools you already use, and hand you the code.

Measure. We check the saving against what we projected, and tell you plainly where it fell short.

FAQ

What people ask before working with us.

Do we need a data team to work with you?

No. We work specifically with businesses that do not have one. That is the gap we fill: the judgement about what is worth automating, not just the build.

Then we tell you that. The plan is the product, and it stays yours whether you build anything with us or not.

No. We build with plain, widely used tools inside the systems you already run, and you own everything we build.
