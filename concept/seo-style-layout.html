01

The short answer

What does an automation partner actually do?

We find where a business loses hours to repeated manual work, and which of those processes are worth automating.

We sit with the people doing the work, map every repeated task, and price it in hours and money. Then we recommend what to automate, in what order, and what it costs to build.

We build with plain, widely used tools, wired into the systems you already run. Some processes we recommend you leave alone, because the hours saved would not cover the build.

Key takeaways
  • We diagnose before we quote. The plan is yours either way.
  • Every recommendation carries a price and an estimated saving.
  • We build inside your tools, and you own the code.
02

How we work

We build the one thing that pays back first.

Every engagement runs in the same order. Skipping a stage is how automation projects end up expensive and unused.

Map. We sit with your team and log every repeated task.

Rank and price. Each process gets an estimated saving and a fixed build price.

Build. We build the workflow that pays back first and hand you the code.

Measure. We check the saving against what we projected, and tell you where it fell short.
