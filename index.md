## What I do.
{: style="margin-bottom:28px"}

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

I work with small and mid-sized businesses that suspect AI or automation could help them but aren't sure where it actually fits. If there's a process in your week that feels dumber than it should be, copying things between systems, chasing the same updates, compiling the same report, send it my way and I'll tell you straight whether it's worth automating and roughly what it would take.

I build with Python, AWS, n8n, OpenRouter - I adapt the stack according to the job.
